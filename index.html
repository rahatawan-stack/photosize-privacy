Privacy Policy for PhotoSize

Developer: R@wan  |  Last Updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoSize ("the App") handles user information.

1. 100% On-Device Image Processing

PhotoSize compresses, resizes, and archives images entirely on your device. Your photos and output files are never uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to any server operated by the developer.

2. Local Preferences & App Data

Preferences such as theme selection, compression history metadata, and ad-removal status are stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time in the app or by uninstalling PhotoSize.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising & In-App Purchases)

PhotoSize uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements and Google Play Billing to process the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. These Google services may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and diagnostic interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

PhotoSize does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us
